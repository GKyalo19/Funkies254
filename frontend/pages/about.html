About Funkies 254

How painful was it when your friend hyped an event so much only to end the conversation with “unfortunately the registration is closed”. Yes, very pinching, I know.

I’m Grace Kyalo, a student at Princeton University and an alumna of Pangani Girls School, Kenya. I love attending events; for the chance to meet and interact with peers, to get mentorship and see what is happening outside of my school bubble. That is why I came up with Funkies254.

Funkies 254 is a place for Kenyan high school and college students to find events. You register on the organiser’s own site.
